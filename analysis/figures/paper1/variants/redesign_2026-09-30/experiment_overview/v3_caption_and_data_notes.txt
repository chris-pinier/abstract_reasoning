Overview variant 3. A: Enlarged task example and parallel human/LLM representational pipelines. Human fixation-locked trial averages yield regional pattern means within session, then session RDMs are averaged within participant and across participants. LLM sequence-token activations are averaged within pattern at a layer selected independently of EEG. Both modalities produce 8-by-8 correlation-distance RDMs. Their 28 unique upper-triangular entries are extracted in the same pattern-pair order and compared with Pearson correlation. Matrices and entry strips are schematic, not measured data.

The band represents uncertainty in the mean, not the spread of individual participant accuracies. Model lines have no uncertainty intervals. Uniform four-choice baseline =25%.
