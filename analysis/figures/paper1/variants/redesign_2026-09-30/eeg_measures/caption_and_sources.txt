Construction of three EEG measures. Pre-trial rest: −600 to 0 ms relative to trial start, during the fixation cross. FRP: 0–600 ms from each retained sequence fixation; epochs are aligned and averaged within trial and may overlap. Response: −600 to 0 ms before response, or response-window end for omissions. Regional electrode averages yield trial time courses. Trial vectors are averaged within pattern and session before computing correlation-distance RDMs. Session RDMs are averaged within participant, then participant RDMs equally across the group. Schematics do not show measured EEG or trial durations.
